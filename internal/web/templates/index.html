Find out what people actually want.

QuickSurvey asks one question well: which of these are you interested in? You write the options, share one link, and everyone gives a thumbs-up to as many as they like.

Got a link from someone? Open it directly — there is nothing to sign up for.

Anonymous by construction

No account, no name, no email. Responses are stored without an IP address or any link to a person — there is nothing in the file to trace back.

One vote per person, gently

A cookie keeps casual double-voting out. Change your mind and re-submit: your new answer replaces the old one instead of stacking on top of it.

Pick as many as you like

Options are not mutually exclusive. Thumbs-up everything that appeals, ignore the rest. Respondents can suggest options too, subject to a moderator.

Results you can actually use

Download a tab-separated file and drop it straight into Google Sheets — one row per response to pivot, or a ready-made tally.
